Privacy declaration

No account. No name fields. Paste the reply as it is. We detect identifying details and show you the public version before you submit.

Only the processed version is saved to the mailbox. Redacted details become black blocks; their original text is not included in the public collection.

Your preview is prepared in your browser. Only the version you confirm is sent to Supabase. The original text is not sent or saved by this submission flow. We use no external AI service.

Rule-based detection can miss names, companies and other identifying information. Review the preview before confirming. You can go back and edit if something was missed.

Submissions stay private until reviewed. We check identifying details before sharing a copy and may remove additional details. Not every contribution will appear in the public collection.

There’s no withdrawal option after submitting. Only send something you’re comfortable contributing. The words remain with their respective rights holders.

This application uses no analytics or cookies. Hosting and database providers may retain technical request logs.
